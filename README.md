## About Pulse

Campus is full of small plans — a chai run, a badminton game, a study session, a movie night, or a shared cab into town.

The problem is often not finding something to do. It's finding the few people who also want to do it.

**Pulse** is designed to solve that problem.

Built for the IIT Kharagpur student community, Pulse lets students discover activities happening around campus, host their own plans, and join activities organized by others.

The product revolves around one simple loop:

**Discover → Join or Host → Coordinate → Meet → Repeat**

## The Problem

**55% of surveyed students reported difficulty finding, joining, and creating meaningful everyday campus activities.**

Existing coordination often happens through scattered WhatsApp groups, informal conversations, or word of mouth, making it difficult to discover relevant activities and find people who are interested at the same time.

Pulse brings this experience into one focused platform.

## Core Features

### Discover Activities

Browse campus activities through a structured activity taxonomy:

- Daily Life
- Recreation
- Creative
- Sports
- Learning & Networking

Activity cards surface key information such as time, location, host, capacity, eligibility, and cost.

### Host an Activity

Students can create their own activities by defining:

- Activity type
- Date and time
- Location
- Capacity
- Eligibility
- Cost
- Description
- Join-request cutoff
- Chat availability

### Join Activities

Students can discover activities and request to join them.

The participation flow is:

**Activity → Join Request → Pending → Host Decision → Participant**

### Activity Management

Hosts can manage their activities and participants, while students can track their hosted and joined activities through the Activity Hub.

### Activity Chat

Activities provide a dedicated space for participants and hosts to coordinate details before and during the activity.

### Shared Materials

Hosts can share relevant documents and materials with participants.

### Notifications & Reminders

The product includes activity-related notifications and reminders to keep participants informed about upcoming activities and changes.

### Profiles & Settings

Students have profiles containing relevant campus information along with privacy and account settings.
